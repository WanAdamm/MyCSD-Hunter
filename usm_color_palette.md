# Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) Color Palette Summary

Colors form a significant and important part of USM's brand identity [cite: document-1.pdf]. Correct usage helps maintain the brand's image and allows the university to be clearly distinguished both in Malaysia and internationally [cite: document-1.pdf].

## Official University Colors
The new USM Brand Identity capitalizes on the strong colors in their Brand Signature [cite: document-1.pdf].

*   **USM Purple**
    *   **Significance:** Signifying high rank, this is the official color of USM [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **Color Values:** Pantone DS 175-1C [cite: document-1.pdf]. CMYK: 80% Cyan, 100% Magenta, 0% Yellow, 30% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
*   **USM Orange**
    *   **Significance:** Taken from the color of the tiger in the official USM crest to signify the dynamic and active nature of USM as a knowledge enterprise at both national and international levels [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **Color Values:** Pantone DS 36-1C [cite: document-1.pdf]. CMYK: 0% Cyan, 65% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].

## USM School Colors
USM devised a clear visual grouping by Schools to give them individual identities while relating them to the overall university [cite: document-1.pdf].

*   **Health (Red)**
    *   **Significance:** Symbolizes the innovative and exploratory horizons chased in efforts to deliver valued scientific research and applications [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **CMYK:** 15% Cyan, 100% Magenta, 90% Yellow, 10% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
*   **Sciences (Green)**
    *   **Significance:** Symbolizes the sustainability of the environment and the promise of youth, balancing the demands of humanity with the sensitivities of the planet [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **CMYK:** 50% Cyan, 0% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
*   **Arts (Magenta)**
    *   **Significance:** Represents the passion, strength, and energy of creative individuals who are the heartbeat of society with a strong desire to succeed [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **CMYK:** 0% Cyan, 100% Magenta, 0% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
*   **Engineering (Cyan)**
    *   **Significance:** Stands for the precision of the experts at USM Engineering and is associated with the highest level of professionalism, intelligence, and confidence [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **CMYK:** 100% Cyan, 0% Magenta, 0% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
*   **Hybrid (Yellow)**
    *   **Significance:** Reflects the melding of Schools and the expanding, innovative approach to holistic education [cite: document-1.pdf].
    *   **CMYK:** 0% Cyan, 20% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].

## USM Crest Color Reproduction
The USM Crest is reproduced using eight specific colors to maintain consistency across all media [cite: document-1.pdf].

1.  **Yellow:** Pantone 7404C, CMYK: 2% Cyan, 9% Magenta, 80% Yellow, 1% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
2.  **Orange:** Pantone 143C, CMYK: 0% Cyan, 41% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
3.  **Dark Orange:** Pantone 144C, CMYK: 0% Cyan, 50% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
4.  **Red:** Pantone 485C, CMYK: 0% Cyan, 100% Magenta, 87% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
5.  **Brown:** Pantone 7516C, CMYK: 24% Cyan, 76% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 15% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
6.  **Green:** Pantone 362C, CMYK: 75% Cyan, 0% Magenta, 100% Yellow, 0% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
7.  **Purple:** Pantone 2617C, CMYK: 87% Cyan, 100% Magenta, 24% Yellow, 12% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
8.  **Black:** CMYK: 50% Cyan, 50% Magenta, 0% Yellow, 100% Black [cite: document-1.pdf].
